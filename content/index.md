I am a Ph.D. candidate in computer science at Cornell University in AI/ML, advised by [Kevin Ellis](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~ellisk/). I received my bachelor's and master's degrees from MIT.

I am interested in the application of neural networks to symbolic domains such as mathematical reasoning, programming, or world modeling. My recent work focuses on algorithms for [symbolic regression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic_regression), including evolutionary algorithms and neural network distillation.
